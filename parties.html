Party questions.

What's the smallest party you'll do?

There's no guest minimum. Pricing starts at our 4-hour minimum for up to 50 guests.

Can kids come?

Of course. They'll love the dirty soda bar. We never serve alcohol to anyone under 21.

Do I need a liquor license?

Nope, not for a private event where you buy the alcohol and nobody pays for drinks. And never for a zero-proof bar. We don't run cash bars.

What happens to the leftover liquor?

It's yours. Every bottle goes home with you at the end of the night.

How much should I buy?

We send you a shopping list built for your guest count. Rule of thumb: about one drink per guest per hour.

Do you charge for travel?

Not in Hillsborough County. Pinellas or Pasco adds a flat $100. Farther than that, we'll quote it.

Can we book a shorter party?

Sure. Two- and three-hour parties are welcome, priced at our 4-hour minimum.

Are you insured?

Yes, general and liquor liability. We'll send your venue a certificate if they ask.

How do I hold the date?

30% deposit and a signed agreement. The rest is due 14 days out. Cancel more than 60 days ahead for a full refund.
