We park a bar at your party, pour well, and make the evening feel easy. You buy the liquor at retail and keep whatever's left. With our trailer packages we bring the bar, the ice, the mixers, premium cups, and bartenders who'd look right in a tux or in boots.

A little saloon on wheels.

The back door drops down into a railed porch. Guests climb a few steps into an air-conditioned lounge and order at a bar that runs across the front of the trailer. The crowd outside gets served through a half door on the side, so nobody waits long for a drink.

For big weddings, add a second bar on the lawn to keep the lines short. We set up anywhere a truck can reach: a pasture, an oak grove, a waterfront lawn, or a venue courtyard. We need about 32 by 10 feet of level ground.

Before you RSVP.

Do I need a liquor license?

Not for a private event where you buy the alcohol and guests aren't charged for drinks. We don't offer cash bars at this time.

What happens to the liquor we don't open?

It's yours. It goes home with you at the end of the night.

How much should I buy?

Every booking comes with a shopping list built for your guest count and hours. A good rule is about one drink per guest per hour.

Can the trailer reach my property?

If a pickup towing a trailer can get there, we usually can. We'll check the spot by photos or a visit before your date.

Are you insured?

Yes, with general and liquor liability coverage. We'll send your venue a certificate on request.

Can we book a shorter party?

Yes. We do two- and three-hour events. They're priced at our 4-hour minimum, the same as a four-hour booking.

Can you make a drink just for us?

Yes. Classic and house drinks are part of every booking. A custom signature cocktail, built and tasted with you, is $125 each.

Do you charge for travel?

Not in Hillsborough County. Events in Pinellas or Pasco add a flat $100. Farther than that, we'll quote it with your date.

How do I hold the date?

A 30% deposit and a signed agreement. The balance is due 14 days out. Cancel more than 60 days ahead for a full refund.
